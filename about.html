About the developer

About Dozelight and its developer.

Dozelight is made by Artur Pereć, an independent developer in Poland. It's a one-person project: no investors, no ads and no tracking in the app.

Updated 25 October 2026.

Why it exists

Artur often works late into the night, wearing glasses that filter blue light. On screen, Night Shift on his Mac and Night Light on Windows helped a little, but they stop at a warm orange and never went as warm as he wanted.

“I wanted my screen to do what my glasses do: go all the way to candlelight and pure red, and get there by itself as bedtime comes closer. Nothing built in did that, so I built Dozelight.”

That is still the idea. Dozelight warms your screen down to 1000K or pure red, and in Auto mode it follows your bedtime, so you don't have to think about it.

Where it is today

The first versions came out on 29 September 2026. Dozelight now runs on Mac and Windows, in English and Polish. On Mac it's signed by its developer and notarized by Apple. On Windows you can also get it from Microsoft Store. Every release is listed in What's new.

Privacy and refunds

The app goes online only to check your license key and to look for updates. There are no ads and no tracking: the privacy policy has the details. If Dozelight isn't right for you, email within 14 days of your purchase for a full refund (see the refund policy).
