Features

Small app. Warm light. Every detail.

Past Night Shift

Night Shift tops out at 2700K. Dozelight adds warmth on top of it, down to 1000K, with presets from Warm to Ember.

Red only

Removes blue and green completely, leaving the gentlest light a screen can make. Dim the red to taste.

Follows your bedtime

Auto mode warms the screen gradually through the evening, switches to red after bedtime and fades back before you wake.

The Touch Bar too

Night Shift ignores the Touch Bar. Dozelight tints it to match your screen, or gives it its own slider.

A pointer you can find

In deep red the arrow gets lost. Turn on a larger pointer and a soft halo, and switch off the keyboard backlight, only when the screen is at its warmest.

Dusk and dawn

Today's dusk and dawn, right in the menu. No location permission needed.

Filters and accessibility

Black & White, Sepia, Invert Colors and Darkroom, plus High Contrast and Extra Dim for going darker than the lowest brightness.

Instant

Reacts to Night Shift, your settings and your displays in a fraction of a second. No polling, no lag.

Featherlight and private

A 1.6 MB download, no measurable CPU when idle and no tracking. It only goes online to check your license and look for updates.

Your night

A screen that winds down with you.

Blue light is what reads as daytime. With Auto mode, Dozelight lowers it little by little as bedtime gets closer, then keeps only red light through the night, so a 3 AM glance at your screen stays gentle.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

iPhone and iPad don't allow apps to tint the whole screen, so there's no Dozelight for them. But they have built-in settings that come close: see our free guide to a red screen at night on iPhone and iPad.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Touch Bar tinting works on Apple silicon MacBook Pros with a Touch Bar.

Does it replace Night Shift?

It works together with it. Keep Night Shift on its usual schedule and Dozelight adds warmth on top of it. In Auto mode Dozelight follows your own bedtime, even when Night Shift is off.

What are Darkroom and Extra Dim?

Darkroom inverts the screen and keeps only red, so pages turn black with red text: the gentlest way to read late at night. Extra Dim makes the screen darker than the lowest brightness setting. Both are in the Filters menu, next to Black & White, Sepia, Invert Colors and High Contrast, and they work at any time of day.

Will it slow down my Mac?

No. Dozelight only does work when something changes, like Night Shift turning on or you moving a slider. When idle it uses no measurable CPU.

Do screenshots and screen recordings look red?

No. The tint is applied by the display itself, so screenshots, recordings and screen sharing keep their true colors.

Does it collect any data?

No. Dozelight has no analytics or tracking. It goes online only to activate and occasionally check your license key, sending just the key and your Mac's name, and once a day to look for updates. Dusk and dawn are estimated from your time zone, so it doesn't need your location either.

Will Dozelight help me sleep better?

It takes away one thing that can push sleep later: bright, blue-rich screen light in the evening. Auto mode warms the screen gradually and keeps only red light after bedtime. Regular sleep times, winding down and a dark bedroom matter at least as much, so see our guide to screens, blue light and better sleep. Dozelight is a comfort tool, not a medical product.

Is it a medical product?

No. Dozelight is a comfort tool for using your Mac in the evening. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

What does early access mean?

Dozelight is complete and ready for everyday use, and new features are on the way. During early access it costs less, and the people using it help decide what comes next. It also isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. Your license keeps working after early access ends, and updates keep coming automatically.

What happens when the trial ends?

The tint pauses until you enter a license key. Your settings stay as they are, so everything comes back the moment you activate.

How do updates work?

Dozelight checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, it shows what's new and installs it with one click. You can also choose Settings → Check for Updates… in its menu at any time.

How do I move my license to a new Mac?

Open License… in Dozelight's menu on the old Mac and click Deactivate This Mac. You can also manage your Macs in the Polar customer portal linked in your receipt.

Dozelight isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Follow the install steps above. Future versions will be notarized.
