Which one should you use?

  • A gentle evening tint is enough: use the built-in Night Shift on Mac or Night Light on Windows. They're free and already there.
  • You want free and much warmer: f.lux is a long-standing free option for personal use, with a schedule around your wake-up time.
  • You want the warmest light, pure red at night, and a schedule around your bedtime: Dozelight for Mac or Dozelight for Windows. It also has Black & White, Sepia, Darkroom and Extra Dim filters, a larger pointer with a halo for deep red, and on Mac, Touch Bar tinting and working together with Night Shift.

How Dozelight is different

On Mac, Dozelight works together with Night Shift instead of replacing it: Night Shift keeps its schedule and Dozelight adds warmth on top. It also tints the Touch Bar, which Night Shift leaves white. On both Mac and Windows, Auto mode warms the screen gradually through the evening, switches to red light after your bedtime and fades back before you wake, and a single license works on up to 3 computers, Mac or Windows.

Questions

Is f.lux free?

f.lux is free for personal use. Businesses need a license from its makers.

Can I use Dozelight and Night Shift together?

Yes. On Mac, Dozelight works with Night Shift on or off; when it's on, Dozelight builds on its warmth. On Windows, turn Night Light off so the two don't compete.

How warm is 1000K?

About the glow of embers. For comparison, candlelight is around 1900K, a classic light bulb around 2700K and daylight around 6500K. Below that Dozelight also offers Red Only, which removes blue and green completely.

Does Dozelight collect data?

No. It has no analytics or tracking. It only goes online to check your license key and look for updates.
