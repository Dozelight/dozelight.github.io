Installation guide

Install Dozelight in five steps.

It takes about a minute. Dozelight isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once, the first time you open it. After that it opens normally, and updates install with one click.

You need macOS 13 Ventura or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac. The pictures below are illustrations, so your screen may look slightly different.

Download Dozelight
  1. 1

    Drag Dozelight to Applications

    Open Dozelight.dmg from your Downloads folder. In the window that appears, drag the Dozelight icon onto the Applications folder.

    Then you can eject the Dozelight disk in the Finder sidebar.

  2. 2

    Open Dozelight, then click Done

    Open Dozelight from your Applications folder or with Spotlight (⌘ Space, then type Dozelight). macOS says it could not verify Dozelight. That's expected for apps that aren't notarized yet.

    Click Done. Don't choose Move to Trash.

  3. 3

    Click Open Anyway in System Settings

    Open System Settings from the Apple menu and choose Privacy & Security in the sidebar. Scroll down to the Security section. Next to the message about Dozelight, click Open Anyway.

    The button appears for about an hour after step 2. If you don't see it, open Dozelight once more and come back.

  4. 4

    Confirm with Open Anyway

    macOS asks once more. Click Open Anyway, then confirm with your Mac's password or Touch ID.

    You only do steps 2 to 4 once. From now on Dozelight opens like any other app.

  5. 5

    Find Dozelight in your menu bar

    Dozelight opens with a short tutorial. Afterwards it lives in the menu bar at the top right of your screen, near Control Center. Look for the sun on the horizon and click it to open the menu.

    Your 14-day free trial starts now, with every feature.

Updates

Dozelight checks for a new version once a day and installs it with one click, with no warning from macOS. You can also choose Settings → Check for Updates… in its menu.

Troubleshooting

I don't see the Open Anyway button

macOS only shows it for about an hour after you try to open Dozelight. Open Dozelight again, click Done, then go straight to System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.

macOS says Dozelight is damaged and can't be opened

The download was probably interrupted. Delete Dozelight from Applications, download it again from this site and repeat the steps. If it still happens, email us at dozelight@icloud.com.

I can't find the icon in the menu bar

If your menu bar is full, macOS may hide some icons behind the camera notch. Quit a few other menu bar apps, or open System Settings → Menu Bar and make sure Dozelight is allowed. You can also open Dozelight from Spotlight at any time: it opens its menu for you.

I'm on an older version of macOS

Dozelight needs macOS 13 Ventura or later. On older versions it won't open.

Uninstalling

Open Dozelight's menu, choose Settings and turn off Open at Login, then choose Quit Dozelight. Your screen, Touch Bar, pointer and keyboard go back to normal right away. Then drag Dozelight from Applications to the Trash.

If you bought a license and want to use it on another Mac, first choose Settings → License… → Deactivate This Mac.
