Guide for Mac

How to make Night Shift warmer on your Mac.

Open System Settings → Displays → Night Shift and drag Color Temperature all the way to More Warm. That is as warm as Night Shift goes, about 2700K. To go warmer, down to candlelight or pure red, use the Color Filters accessibility setting for a red tint, or an app like Dozelight, which adds warmth on top of Night Shift down to 1000K and follows your bedtime.

Set Night Shift as warm as it goes

  1. Open Night ShiftChoose Apple menu → System Settings → Displays, then click Night Shift… at the bottom.
  2. Go to More WarmDrag Color Temperature all the way to the right, to More Warm.
  3. Schedule itChoose Sunset to Sunrise, or Custom to pick your own hours.
  4. Switch it quicklyNight Shift is also in Control Center → Display, for turning it on right now.

Color Filters is in System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Color Filters. Turn it on, choose Color Tint as the filter type, pick red and raise the intensity. It works, but it tints everything evenly, has no schedule, and you switch it on and off by hand. Reduce White Point on the same page makes the screen dimmer, which helps too.

Questions

Can Night Shift go below 2700K?

No. More Warm is the limit of the Night Shift slider. Going warmer takes the Color Filters accessibility setting or a separate app like Dozelight.

Is Night Shift the same as True Tone?

No. True Tone matches the screen to the color of the light in your room, all day. Night Shift makes the screen warmer in the evening. You can use both.

Is red light better at night?

Research suggests that the blue part of light has the strongest effect on melatonin, the hormone that helps you fall asleep, and red light the weakest. That's why the warmest settings keep mostly red. Dozelight and Night Shift are comfort tools, not medical products.

Do screenshots turn orange?

No. Night Shift and Dozelight change the color on the way to the display, so screenshots, recordings and screen sharing keep their true colors.

Is there something similar for Windows?

Yes. Windows has Night Light, which also stops at a warm orange. See how to make your screen red at night on Windows.
