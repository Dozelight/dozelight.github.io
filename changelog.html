What's new in Dozelight.

The latest versions are:
Dozelight 1.4.6 for Mac (30 September 2026)

Every change to both apps is listed below, newest first.

Updated 29 September 2026.

Dozelight for Mac

Dozelight 1.4.6 30 September 2026

  • The switch always shows whether Dozelight is doing anything, the tutorial has a Not Now choice, and the menu no longer shifts when Auto or Red Only is ticked.

Dozelight 1.4.5 30 September 2026

  • The switch follows the slider: all the way left turns Dozelight off, and moving the slider or turning on a feature turns it on.

Dozelight 1.4.4 30 September 2026

  • The slider all the way left (0%) now adds no tint at all, as if Dozelight were off: the Touch Bar stays white.

Dozelight 1.4.3 30 September 2026

  • Fixed: the Touch Bar could stay tinted after turning Dozelight off.

Dozelight 1.4.2 29 September 2026

  • Switching a setting in the menu keeps the menu open, so you can try several without reopening it
  • The Touch Bar keeps its tint after you lower the warmth
  • One license works on up to 3 computers, Mac or Windows

Dozelight 1.4.1 29 September 2026

  • Black & White and Sepia now work on all current Macs (they use the macOS accessibility grayscale filter and restore your own setting afterwards)

Dozelight 1.4.0 29 September 2026

  • New Filters menu: Black & White, Sepia, Invert Colors and Darkroom (inverted red for night reading)
  • High Contrast for easier reading
  • Extra Dim: go darker than the lowest brightness

Dozelight 1.3.3 29 September 2026

  • Faster and lighter: the menu and slider respond up to 60 times faster
  • Dozelight no longer wakes up during automatic brightness changes

Dozelight 1.3.2 29 September 2026

  • No more flash of normal colors when you turn the brightness all the way down and back up: the screen now fades in already tinted

Dozelight 1.3.1 29 September 2026

  • The tint comes back right away after you turn the screen brightness all the way down and up again

Dozelight 1.3.0 29 September 2026

  • Automatic updates: Dozelight now checks for new versions daily and installs them with one click
  • Check for Updates… in the Settings menu

Dozelight 1.2 29 September 2026

  • The first early-access versions: warmth past Night Shift down to 1000K, presets, Red Only, Auto mode for your bedtime, dusk and dawn, a tutorial, and Touch Bar, pointer and keyboard extras

Dozelight for Windows

Dozelight 1.3.9 30 September 2026

  • Installing an update now shows its progress, explains what went wrong if it didn't work, and tells you when it's done.

Dozelight 1.3.8 30 September 2026

  • Easier on your battery: Dozelight rests while the screen is off or your PC is locked, and when it's switched off.

Dozelight 1.3.7 30 September 2026

  • The switch follows the slider: all the way left turns Dozelight off, and moving the slider or turning on a feature turns it on.

Dozelight 1.3.6 30 September 2026

  • Even lighter: changes to the warmth take about half the work they did before.

Dozelight 1.3.5 30 September 2026

  • Lighter on your PC: uses less than half the memory in the tray and much less CPU while you drag the slider.

Dozelight 1.3.4 30 September 2026

  • Screen readers now hear page changes, tutorial steps, new bedtimes and license errors as they happen.
  • With a larger Windows text size the panel grows fully and scrolls when it doesn't fit the screen.

Dozelight 1.3.3 30 September 2026

  • Works with screen readers such as Narrator: every control, its state and the page text can be read and used.
  • Full keyboard control: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space presses them, arrow keys move sliders, with a visible focus outline.
  • Follows Windows high contrast themes and the Text size setting, and keeps all text clearly readable with any accent color.

Dozelight 1.3.2 29 September 2026

  • Version numbers now start at 1, like the Mac app. The app itself is the same as 0.3.2.

Dozelight 0.3.2 29 September 2026

  • A short setup tutorial on first launch, also in Settings → Show Tutorial
  • Click a settings page's title to go back, not just the arrow
  • Updates are signed, so only genuine Dozelight updates install
  • Unlock Warmer Colors is more robust

Dozelight 0.3.1 29 September 2026

  • No more flicker when you change the tint while Windows limits the warmest colors

Dozelight 0.3.0 29 September 2026

  • Auto mode: warmer through the evening, red after bedtime, back to normal before you wake
  • Sleep schedule, and today's dusk and dawn in the panel
  • Filters: Black & White, Sepia, Invert Colors, Darkroom, High Contrast and Extra Dim
  • Larger pointer and a halo behind it in deep red
  • Snap the slider to presets
  • Automatic updates, and settings on their own pages in the panel

Dozelight 0.2.0 29 September 2026

  • 14-day free trial, and license keys that work on both Mac and Windows

Dozelight 0.1.0 29 September 2026

  • First preview: the tray icon and panel, the warmth slider, presets, Red Only and Open at Login

Dozelight checks for new versions once a day and installs them with one click, on Mac and Windows. You can also choose Check for Updates in its settings.
