Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who we are

Dozelight is run by Artur Pereć in Poland, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at dozelight@icloud.com.

The Mac app

Dozelight has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your settings stay on your Mac. Dusk and dawn are estimated from your time zone, so the app never asks for your location.

The app goes online for two things only. To activate and occasionally check your license key, it sends your license key and your Mac's name to Polar (polar.sh), which runs our store. Your Mac's name is shown to you in the Polar customer portal, so you can see and manage your activations.

Once a day it also checks this website for a new version of Dozelight, using the open-source Sparkle updater. That request contains no personal data beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, and it goes to GitHub Pages, which hosts this website.

The Windows app

Dozelight for Windows has no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and your settings stay in your Windows user account. It goes online only to activate and occasionally check your license key, sending your license key and your PC's name to Polar in the same way as the Mac app. The version downloaded from this website also checks it once a day for a new version, in the same way as the Mac app. The version from Microsoft Store doesn't: Microsoft Store keeps it up to date.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Polar as the merchant of record, which means Polar sells Dozelight to you on our behalf and processes your payment. Polar handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your order details, such as your email address, country and what you bought, so we can provide your license and support. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates on our website, we store your email address with Formspree, the service that collects the sign-ups, and use it only to send you news about Dozelight. The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we will delete your address. We keep it until then.

Support

If you email us, we use your message and email address to help you and keep the conversation only as long as it is useful for that.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The website sets no cookies and loads its fonts from this site rather than from third parties.

To know how many people visit the website and download Dozelight, it counts page views and clicks on the download and Buy buttons with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It uses no cookies, doesn't store your IP address and can't follow you across websites. What it keeps is the page, the website that sent you here, and your browser, operating system, screen size and country. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used. The apps themselves don't use it.

Transfers outside the EU

Polar, Formspree and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area. They rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Just email us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
